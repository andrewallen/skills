# Entity research map template

Use for `<Entity Name> - Research Map.md` beside a canonical person or organisation profile, mainly in Word-plus-map collections. The map is navigation, not the substantive account or independent corroboration. Keep taxonomy wording identical across entities, and keep restricted analysis in its authorised location rather than duplicating it here.

```markdown
---
document_role: entity-research-map
template: entity-research-map@1.0
created_by: stakeholder-governance@<CURRENT_SKILL_VERSION>
last_governed_by: stakeholder-governance@<CURRENT_SKILL_VERSION>
---

# Entity Name - Research Map

Entity type: <person | organisation>
Supports: <relative link to the canonical Person Profile or Organisation Profile>, which holds the substantive account.
Map review scope: <range, cutoff, date>. Profile review scope: <same, or state the difference>.

Taxonomy: **Captured** original record; **Extracted** quotation, fact or close paraphrase; **Synthesised** organised or connected statements; **Inferred** interpretation; **Proposed** our recommendation.

## Coverage

| Source surface | Owner / existing archive link | Status | Range or records checked | Material gap or handoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Profile sections

<Use the matching person or organisation profile headings. For each, record source pointers, anchors, attribution cautions and provenance boundaries.>
```

Replace `<CURRENT_SKILL_VERSION>` when creating the map. Preserve `created_by`. Update `last_governed_by` only after a material update or validation.

A map-only update is not a profile refresh; do not advance the profile's review cutoff because the map changed.
