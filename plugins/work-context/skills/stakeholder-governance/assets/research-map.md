# Entity research map template

Use for `<Entity Name> - Research Map.md` beside a canonical person or organisation profile, mainly in Word-plus-map collections. The map is navigation, not the substantive account or independent corroboration. Keep taxonomy wording identical across entities, and keep restricted analysis in its authorised location rather than duplicating it here.

last_governed_by: stakeholder-governance@<CURRENT_SKILL_VERSION>
---
